# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners, executive directors, and sales and marketing leads at California assisted living and memory care operators with 2–20 communities. Busy, not technical, often older, and burned before by agencies and clunky software. They read this page between tours and staff issues, on a phone, iPad or office desktop.

## Product Purpose

Kinlight (working name, from Catapult) gives a senior living community a website that is always current and keeps every family in the loop, from one place. Staff post once; it reaches the website, families' texts and inboxes, and large-print paper for residents. This site's one job: get operators to click Book a meeting. The offer sits on the first screen, and an Only 5 spots banner tops every page. How many spots are left is deliberately not shown.

## Positioning

"Website companies publish to the web. Kinlight publishes to people." Against engagement apps: we make it public. Against website agencies: we keep it alive, and the site belongs to the operator. Backed by Catapult, which has built and run websites since 2000. On the site, say Catapult hosts hundreds of websites and deliberately leave out that they are K-12 (user's direction, 30 Oct 2026); link every mention to catapultcms.com.

## Operating Context

The product is in development. Pilot from March 2027, public launch July 2027. Features are claimed only if in the alpha feature list or pilot offer; anything later is labelled "coming". Facts come from the read-only project vault, with the Decision Log winning.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product prices never appear on the site, only the offer (user's direction, 30 Oct 2026). Internal plan prices live in the vault.
- Forms on this site validate and thank but send nothing yet.
- Site stays unlisted (noindex) until the name is trademark-checked and agreed.
- Never on the page: finances, equity, prospect names, competitor names, meeting notes, open questions, unverified facts.

## Brand Commitments

Logo: the Kinlight Amber Glow logo in public/brand, navy lettering with an amber dot over the i. Light backgrounds only. A separate K icon is the tab and home-screen icon. Voice: direct and informative, simple enough for a 12-year-old, short sentences, benefits first, no jargon, no filler (Hormozi-style clarity, without promises the vault can't back). The look is technical and professional, in the logo's navy and amber (user's direction); amber is an accent, never a full section of saturated yellow. The words stay human.

## Evidence on Hand

Catapult's track record and the founding offer. No customers, testimonials or logos exist and none may be invented. Product illustrations use a clearly fictional sample community.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the operator's problem, not the website.
- Every page leads to one action: Book a meeting.
- Every promise on the page is one the team can keep today or is labelled as coming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA at minimum. Buyers and families skew older: large type, high contrast, generous targets, keyboard navigation, browser zoom for larger text (the on-page A/A+ control was removed at the user's request), reduced-motion support.
