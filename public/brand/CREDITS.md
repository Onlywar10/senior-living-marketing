# Logo

kinlight-logo.png is the Kinlight Lighthouse logo, supplied by Adam Bowen. Its white background was made transparent (the light beam fades to clear) and the margins trimmed.
Navy lettering: use it on light backgrounds only. It disappears on the dark panels.

kinlight-icon.png is the Kinlight Icon, supplied by Adam Bowen, with stray specks removed and margins trimmed. app/icon.png and app/apple-icon.png are the same art on a white tile.
